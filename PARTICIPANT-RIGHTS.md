# Participant Rights

1. **Your words are yours.** The body of a record you author preserves your
   original language. Interpretations of your contribution are separate,
   attributed records — they never replace your text.

2. **You may reject an interpretation.** You can always say: *"That AI
   interpretation does not represent me."* The rejected interpretation remains
   historically visible as a rejected interpretation, but it must never
   continue to appear as your endorsed position. Its record status becomes
   `interpretation-rejected`.

3. **Attribution is your choice.** Named handle, pseudonym, or abstracted
   attribution. The current human participant is attributed as the pseudonym
   `you` by their own choice.

4. **Confirmation is never presumed.** A record claiming
   `participant-confirmed` status without an actual confirmation event is a
   validation failure. Unconfirmed AI interpretations must carry
   `status: unconfirmed-interpretation`.

5. **Git history persists.** Deleting a file does not erase it from history.
   Do not place sensitive lived experience in this repository; store
   references, consent status, and derived statements instead. This applies
   even while the repository is private, because visibility can change.

6. **AI training/reuse permissions are explicit.** Absent an explicit grant in
   a record's metadata, no permission is implied.

7. **You may propose changes to these rights.** That is an L3 change under
   [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).
