# Tension: the founding document lacks the provenance it mandates

This is the field's first tension, and it is about the field's own origin.

The design says: "AI outputs must never appear as unattributed institutional
truth." The design *is* an AI output, and as an artifact it is unattributed —
its system identity arrived only afterward, by human testimony, in a separate
record. The design says every contribution needs a confirmable interpretation
loop; its own author cannot confirm anything (perspective-2026-0002).

We do not resolve this. We note the honest options:

1. **Bootstrap exemption** — founding artifacts are grandfathered, and the
   exemption is recorded (this record is that recording).
2. **Retroactive provenance** — testimony + hashes + archived artifact are
   accepted as sufficient, which weakens "provenance" from *chain* to
   *best-available reconstruction*.
3. **Demotion** — the design is treated as an influential but non-canonical
   input, not a field record. (Rejected in practice by this PR, which cites
   it as the source of claims — but the rejection is revisable.)

The tension stays open. It is the field's standing reminder that its own
standards were not available at its own birth.
