# Tension: pre-specified grammar vs. L3 emergence

Sections 2 and 5 of the design hand the field a complete ontology of thirteen
object kinds and twenty relation types before any participant has contributed
anything. Section 16 says the field's generativity consists in participants
changing that grammar. Both cannot be fully honored at the seed: every kind
we pre-specify is a kind nobody proposed through tension.

This PR took a middle path, deliberately: schema v0.1.0 includes only the
kinds actually *used* by seed records plus the design's relation vocabulary
verbatim (so deviations are visible as diffs, not silent edits). Everything
else — bridges, experiments, syntheses in practice — waits for a real need.

The formal note: in the Lean model, `l3_extends_expressible` proves grammar
steps can strictly enlarge what is expressible. The tension is that F0's
expressible set was fixed by fiat. The theorem guarantees the door is real;
it cannot guarantee we started in the right room.
