# Claim: generativity = structural self-modification under scrutiny

> "The field becomes generative when contributions can modify not just its
> contents but its structure."

The three-level picture (L1 contributions, L2 relations/interpretations,
L3 grammar changes) is formalized in the Lean development: the field's
evolution is modeled as a step relation in which L3 steps change the schema
that subsequent records are validated against. See
`verification/lean/GenerativeField/Basic.lean` — in particular the theorem
that a grammar step can make previously ill-formed records well-formed
(`l3_extends_expressible`), which is the formal content of "changes what the
system is capable of seeing."
