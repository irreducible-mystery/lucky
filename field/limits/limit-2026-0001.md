# Limit: the unavailable half of the origin

The design defines a limit as "an undecidable, uncomputable, unavailable, or
currently untranslated region." This one is of the *unavailable* kind, and it
sits at the field's root: we hold the answer but not the questions.

Consequences the field should carry forward:

- Every extraction from the founding design is an interpretation of a
  response whose eliciting context is unknown. Sol's emphases may be
  artifacts of prompts we cannot see.
- The apparent single-authorship of the design ("I would build it…") may
  understate the human's contribution, which is invisible here. Attribution
  of the design's ideas *between* the two co-authors is undecidable from the
  artifact alone.
