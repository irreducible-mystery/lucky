# Assumption map

*Generated projection — which records stand on which assumptions.*

## assumption-2026-0001

Git/GitHub history is sufficiently trustworthy and durable to serve as the field's memory and provenance layer — commits, reviews, and PR history are treated as an adequate record of who said and decided what, when.

- depended on by `claim-2026-0001`

## assumption-2026-0002

Meaning survives structuring: a participant's contribution can be carried into typed records (YAML front matter, schema'd kinds, typed relations) without losing what mattered about it — provided the original language is preserved alongside and confirmation is sought.

- depended on by `claim-2026-0001`

## assumption-2026-0003

A grammar must already exist before the field can propose changes to its grammar: L3 generativity presupposes an initial schema (F0) that was not itself produced by the field's own review process.

- depended on by `claim-2026-0002`
